Why Prose Screening and Physical Verification Are Complementary

Statistical text classifiers provide an informative upstream signal for tracking population-level authoring drift without penalizing formulaic human protocols. However, altering scalar execution parameters (volumes, temperatures, durations) or appending fabricated citations and physically impossible bench steps leaves surface text probability essentially unchanged. Reliable governance in AI-directed laboratories therefore pairs upstream provenance checks with downstream deterministic deck-state simulation, physical-layer verification, and run-reconstruction provenance at the instrument boundary.
