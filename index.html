Porcupine Labs

Execution-layer red teaming and physical-layer risk strategy for autonomous laboratory ecosystems.

About the Lab

Based in the Bay Area and originating from Catalyze Impact[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span), Porcupine Labs is an independent research organization focused on the governance of automated scientific infrastructure.

As artificial intelligence systems increasingly direct physical laboratory hardware, safety cannot rely solely on upstream text screening or model-layer refusals[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span). We research and develop stateful verification methodologies to secure physical actuation pathways against novel failure modes[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

Our Focus

We investigate how automated facilities record, reconstruct, and verify completed experimental runs[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span). By bridging the gap between digital instructions and physical execution, our work supports transparent, robust standards for the future of autonomous research[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
