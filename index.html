Porcupine Labs

Execution-layer red teaming and physical-layer risk strategy for autonomous laboratory ecosystems.

1. About the Lab

Based in the Bay Area and originating from Catalyze Impact[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span), Porcupine Labs is an independent research organization focused on the governance and safety of automated scientific infrastructure[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

2. The Execution Gap

As artificial intelligence systems increasingly direct physical laboratory hardware, traditional safety checks that evaluate upstream text or intent cannot reach the precise moment a machine moves liquid or material[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

3. Physical-Layer Risk

We research and develop stateful verification methodologies to secure physical actuation pathways against novel failure modes, operating independently of standard model-layer benchmarks[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

4. Reconstructability

We investigate how automated facilities record, reconstruct, and verify completed experimental runs, establishing robust audit trails for complex scientific workflows[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

5. Standards & Governance

Our work supports transparent, rigorous, and practical standards for autonomous research facilities, aligning technical measurements with institutional oversight[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
